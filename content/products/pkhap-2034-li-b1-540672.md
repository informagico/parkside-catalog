20 V SDS-Plus rotary hammer for hammer drilling in masonry, concrete and stone, chiselling in concrete, stone and plaster, and drilling in stone, wood and metal. Features two electronically selected speed levels, reversible rotation, adjustable chisel position and an LED work light. The auxiliary handle rotates through 360° and a metal depth stop controls drilling depth.

Supplied with 6, 8 and 10 mm Performance drill bits, 50 g of grease, a cloth and a carrying case. Battery and charger are sold separately.
