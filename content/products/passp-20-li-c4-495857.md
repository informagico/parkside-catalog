20 V cordless impact wrench with a ½-inch square drive, three electronically selected performance levels and a variable-speed trigger. Delivers up to 1356 Nm torque, 1550 rpm and 1900 impacts per minute. Features clockwise/counter-clockwise rotation, an LED work light and a belt clip. The manual lists 17, 19 and 21 mm sockets, a ½-inch to ¾-inch socket adapter and a storage case.
