20 V SDS-Plus rotary hammer for hammer drilling in masonry, concrete and stone, chiselling in concrete, stone and plaster, and drilling in wood and metal. Features two speed levels, adjustable chisel position, an LED work light, an auxiliary handle and a metal depth stop. Supplied with three drill bits and a carrying case; battery and charger are sold separately.
