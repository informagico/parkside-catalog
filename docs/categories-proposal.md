# Category proposal — awaiting maintainer approval

This is a proposal, not an applied reclassification. The active exact values remain in content/categories.json.

Use one primary category based on the tool's main function. Avoid mixing materials or project settings into the same level: a drill may be used in a workshop, a garden or a construction project.

| Proposed canonical English category | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Gardening | Lawn, hedge and tree care, irrigation and garden equipment |
| Drilling & Fastening | Drills, hammer drills, screwdrivers, impact drivers and wrenches |
| Sawing & Cutting | Saws, cutters and cutting tools |
| Sanding & Grinding | Sanders, grinders, polishers and sharpening equipment |
| Cleaning & Extraction | Vacuums, dust extractors, pressure washers and cleaning equipment |
| Welding & Soldering | Welders, soldering stations and related tools |
| Measuring & Detection | Laser levels, measuring tools, detectors and inspection devices |
| Workshop Equipment | Workbenches, clamping, compressors, inflators, lifting and workshop support equipment |
| Batteries & Accessories | Batteries, chargers, separate attachments and consumables |

A complete kit follows its main tool; a separately cataloged battery, charger or consumable goes under Batteries & Accessories. A combination tool follows its primary marketed function. If that is unclear, ask the maintainer before assigning a category.

Background: official Lidl DIY & Garden navigation distinguishes tools, garden and workshop/hardware, but mixes product types and use cases. The above list is a proposed editorial taxonomy for this independent catalog, not an official Parkside classification.
