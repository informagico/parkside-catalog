# Catalog review — 23 October 2026

The review covered all nine approved categories and updated 440 product records. IANs and existing product URLs were preserved.

- Recovered 74 previously unknown model codes and corrected four existing codes using product manuals.
- Added 326 technical specification values, with per-field PDF page references.
- Identified 100 additional declared manufacturers from conformity declarations for the relevant IANs.
- Added 62 official product photos and eight manuals with matching IANs.
- Removed two component-only manuals and corrected two functional classifications.
- Separated unrelated products with reused model codes in variant comparisons.

A PDF containing an IAN is not sufficient by itself: its subject must also match the record. Combined manuals, accessory documents and ambiguous model lists were excluded from automatic attribution. Existing specification values were preserved in this pass.
