# Manual manufacturer review — 25 October 2026

Reviewed all 745 catalog records that had a linked manual and an unknown manufacturer at commit 2876984. The review covered 910 same-IAN PDF candidates (95,298 PDF pages), including alternate language editions.

Added 486 documentary manufacturer attributions; 259 records remain unknown. Existing attributions, OEMs, model codes, categories, specifications, descriptions, images and manual links are unchanged.

## Method

Read every PDF page, including final declarations. Repaired extraction from custom fonts, applied local OCR to scanned declarations and checked representative original pages and exceptional cases visually. A company is attributed from a manufacturer identification or an original declaration covering the specific IAN and product. This records the declared manufacturer, without identifying an undocumented factory or OEM.

An importer address, service provider, warranty issuer, unlabeled footer or component manufacturer alone is insufficient. Model variants must all be covered by documentary evidence for the same IAN. No evidence was transferred between IANs. Generic conformity statements naming an importer were held back.

Source links below use one-based PDF page numbers. Research references remain optional metadata and this report; product descriptions and product-page Identity sections retain their existing presentation. Italian documentary metadata is aligned with the English records under the maintainer’s existing translation and publication authorization.

## Specific scope and legibility limitations

- IAN 345867: declarations for other air tools do not identify the cataloged blow gun and accessories.
- IAN 494753: a declaration identifies ROWI for PDTS 6.3 C4, but the record also includes other pneumatic tools.
- IANs 345881, 365023, 373204 and 373118: declarations do not cover every listed model.
- IAN 488979: the welding hand-shield declaration does not establish the maker of the entire unspecified accessory assortment.
- IANs 495494, 466525, 495496, 508106 and 492624: importer/conformity statements are insufficient; named cell manufacturers apply to the cells only.
- Some declaration pages are blank or too degraded to establish an attribution, including those encountered for IANs 495466 and 485601. These remain unknown.
- IANs 508234 and 495847: declarations in their own manuals identify PWD 12 B1 and PWD 20 C3 respectively; KAT is a suffix in the existing catalog model strings.
